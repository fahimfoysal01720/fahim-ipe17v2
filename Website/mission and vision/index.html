Mission and Vision

1. We want to design an online platform through which students, city intruders, employees, tourists can find their desired residence to stay. Hotels, messes, hostels, rental homes, rest houses will be included in our service.
2. At our platform, consumers will be able to enjoy residential benefits through proper monitoring and verification according to their needs and capabilities.
3. Owners will be able to register their mess, rental home, hotel and provide all the information related to their accommodation through our platform
4. In our service we will manage & update the data of the residences and tenants.
5. Our platform will facilitate online transactions (through bKash, Nogod, Dbbl, Visa card, Credit card etc.) for both the tenants & owners which will ultimately reduce their time and effort.
6. Our platform will also provide promotional services & advertisement of catering, land sale etc.
